This knowledge base covers the introductory geology course GEOL 101 (Principles of Geology) as taught by Dr. Brandi Lenz. **Fourteen modules** have been ingested, spanning the full arc of the course — from the scientific method and Earth's interior, through the rock-forming processes, to the surface systems (water, rivers, floods, ice, wind, gravity, and waves) that shape the landscape, and finally to the atmosphere and the climate system that links them all.

## The Big Picture

The course is built on two great organizing ideas that together explain almost every topic:

1. **Plate tectonics** — Earth's internal heat engine. It explains why continents move, why ocean basins exist, and why mountains, earthquakes, and volcanoes occur where they do (Modules 2, 4, 5, 7, 9).
2. **The hydrologic cycle and surface processes** — Earth's external engine, powered by the Sun and gravity. Weathering breaks rock down; gravity (mass movement), running water, groundwater, glaciers, wind, and waves erode, transport, and deposit it; and these same systems are both society's most vital resources and its most pervasive natural hazards (Modules 6, 10, 11, 12, 13).

Linking the two are the **rock cycle** (Modules 1, 6, 7) and the immense span of **geologic time** (Module 8) over which these slow processes operate. Overarching both is the **climate system** (Module 14) — the interactive exchange of energy and moisture among the atmosphere, hydrosphere, geosphere, biosphere, and cryosphere — which both shapes and is shaped by geologic processes.

## Key Conceptual Chains

**Silica controls everything (igneous/volcanic):**
- High SiO₂ → felsic/granitic → high viscosity → explosive eruption → composite volcano → most dangerous.
- Low SiO₂ → mafic/basaltic → low viscosity → effusive eruption → shield volcano → less dangerous.

**Tectonic setting → volcano type:**
- Subduction zone → water-induced melting → andesitic/rhyolitic magma → composite/caldera.
- Mid-ocean ridge → decompression melting → basaltic magma → shield volcano / flood basalt.
- Hot spot → basaltic (oceanic, Hawai‘i) or rhyolitic (continental, Yellowstone) → shield chain or caldera.

**Magma evolution (Bowen's Reaction Series):**
- Fractional crystallization removes early minerals (olivine, pyroxene) → residual melt grows richer in silica.

**Stress → deformation (tectonics):**
- Compression → reverse/thrust faults and folds; tension → normal faults; shear → strike-slip faults; convergence builds mountains (orogenesis).

**Surface processes (weathering → erosion → deposition):**
- Weathering supplies sediment → streams and groundwater transport it → deposition builds floodplains, deltas, and alluvial fans → burial and lithification make sedimentary rock (the clastic half of the rock cycle).

**The water connection (hydrology):**
- Precipitation splits between **runoff** (feeding [[Streams and Rivers]]) and **infiltration** (recharging [[Groundwater]]). Groundwater dissolves limestone to build [[Karst Topography]], sustains streams between storms, and — like surface water — is finite and vulnerable to [[Water Pollution and Contamination]]. When discharge exceeds channel capacity, the result is a flood (the most common natural disaster).
