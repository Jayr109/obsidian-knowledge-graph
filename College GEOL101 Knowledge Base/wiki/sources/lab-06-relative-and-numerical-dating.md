Laboratory worksheet applying the principles of relative dating to outcrop photographs and geologic cross-sections, then calculating numerical ages from parent–daughter isotope ratios. Corresponds to Chapter 8 of the laboratory manual (activity sheets 8.1, 8.2, 8.4; textbook pages 221–226). Scanned image-only PDF. All concepts map onto [[Geologic Time]].

## Activities Covered

### Activity 8.1 — Geologic Inquiry for Relative Dating (pp. 221–222)
Introduces **contacts** — the surfaces between rock bodies — using a layer cake, then applies the idea to two natural outcrops.

- **Fig. A8.1.2** (Sedona, Arizona): red paleosol at the base is oldest by **superposition**; fractures cutting the lava flow are younger by **cross-cutting relationships**; lava clasts inside the brown soil are older than the soil by **inclusions**.
- **Fig. A8.1.3**: folded red sandstone beneath horizontal tan conglomerate. By **original horizontality** the sandstone was deposited flat and folded later. The surface between them is an **angular unconformity**: deposit → fold/tilt → uplift and erode → resume deposition.

### Activity 8.2 — Determining Sequence of Events in Geologic Cross-Sections (pp. 223–224)
Four cross-sections to be ordered oldest → youngest using the key to symbols. Letters label both rock **units** and **surfaces** (unconformities, faults); surfaces are the ones drawn with leader lines.

**Cross Section 1** — `E, P, G, L, C, H, O, M, D, J, A, S, N, K, B, R, F`
Granite basement (E) is overlain across a **nonconformity** (P); the tilted G–L–C set is cut by fault H, then beveled by **angular unconformity** O. Granite **A intrudes** J and the older units — proven by the **contact-metamorphism halos** baked into the limestone along its margins — and is then truncated by unconformity S. N–K–B follow, then unconformity R, then surface layer F.

**Cross Section 2 (Grand Canyon)** — `K, J, S, M, E, L, D, C, B, A, F, R, I, H, G`
Vishnu Schist (K) intruded by Zoroaster Granite (J), beveled by a **nonconformity** (S), overlain by basal Gravel (M) and the tilted Grand Canyon Supergroup: Bass Dolomite (E) → Hakatai Shale (L) → Shinumo Quartzite (D) → Dox Sandstone (C) → Chuar Shales (B) → Nankoweap Group (A). A Basalt dike/sill (F) cuts these and is truncated by the **Great Unconformity** (R), above which lie Tapeats Sandstone (I), Bright Angel Shale (H), and Muav Limestone (G).

**Cross Section 3** — `F, P, G, C, B, R, H, A, M, O, E, X, D, K, Z, J, N`
Schist (F) intruded by granite (P) — proven by a **schist xenolith inside the granite** — then fault G, nonconformity C, the folded B–R–H–A sequence, dike M, angular unconformity O, then E–X–D, fault K, unconformity Z, surface layer J, and finally channel N incised through J.

**Cross Section 4** — not fully resolved; see Notes below.

The closing discussion asks which laws of relative dating would be needed to reconstruct the rock layers eroded away to form Grand Canyon Gorge — chiefly **lateral continuity** (beds once extended across the gap) together with superposition and original horizontality.

### Activity 8.4 — Numerical Dating of Rocks and Fossils (p. 226)
Parent–daughter isotope calculations.

| Item | Answer |
|---|---|
| Zircon, 71% U-235 remaining | ≈ **0.5 half-lives** |
| Age of that lava flow | 0.49 × 704 Ma ≈ **350 million years** |
| Layers above / below the flow | Younger than / older than ~350 Ma |
| Meteorite with equal U-238 and Pb-206 | Exactly **1 half-life = 4.5 billion years** — Earth's age |
| Peat with 6% of modern C-14 | ≈ 4.06 half-lives × 5730 yr ≈ **23,000 years** |

Contamination in the peat breaks the **closed-system requirement**: young roots add modern C-14 (age reads too young); old limestone contributes dead carbon (age reads too old).

Detrital zircons on a modern beach date their **source rock's crystallization**, not the beach — the rule being to date minerals that formed when the rock itself formed, using fresh unweathered samples.

The "authentic dinosaur bone" scenario fails on two independent grounds: **carbon-14 cannot reach 400 Ma** (useful below ~70,000 yr), and 400 Ma is Devonian — **before dinosaurs existed** (Mesozoic, ~250–66 Ma).
