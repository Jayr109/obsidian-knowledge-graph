Igneous rocks form from the cooling and crystallization of magma (intrusive) or lava (extrusive). They are classified by **texture** (reflecting cooling rate) and **composition** (reflecting magma chemistry).

## Two Origins
- **Intrusive (plutonic)**: crystallizes slowly at depth → **phaneritic** (coarse-grained) texture; visible crystals. Exposed at surface after uplift and erosion.
- **Extrusive (volcanic)**: solidifies rapidly at or near Earth's surface from lava or volcanic debris → fine-grained, glassy, or vesicular textures.

## Composition Groups

| Group | SiO₂ | Intrusive Rock | Extrusive Rock | Color | Density |
|-------|------|----------------|----------------|-------|---------|
| Felsic (granitic) | 66–76% | Granite | Rhyolite | Light | Low |
| Intermediate (andesitic) | 52–66% | Diorite | Andesite | Gray | Medium |
| Mafic (basaltic) | 45–52% | Gabbro | Basalt | Dark | High |
| Ultramafic | 38–45% | Peridotite | (rare) | Very dark/green | Very high |

Quick rule: **light color = felsic; dark color = mafic**.

## Texture Types

| Texture | Grain size | Cooling | Setting |
|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Pegmatitic | Exceptionally coarse | Extremely slow | Deep intrusive (late-stage) |
| Phaneritic | Coarse (visible) | Slow | Intrusive |
| Porphyritic | Mixed (phenocrysts + groundmass) | Slow then fast | Two-stage |
| Aphanitic | Fine (microscopic) | Fast | Extrusive |
| Vesicular | Void-filled | Very fast | Extrusive |
| Glassy | Amorphous | Extremely fast | Extrusive |
| Pyroclastic | Fragmental | Explosive eruption | Extrusive |

- **Porphyritic**: large crystals (**phenocrysts**) in a finer matrix (**groundmass**). Called a **porphyry**.
- **Pyroclastic**: rock formed from consolidation of explosive ejecta.

## Named Rocks — Quick Reference

**Felsic:**
- **Granite**: phaneritic; K-feldspar + quartz; most abundant intrusive rock.
- **Rhyolite**: aphanitic equivalent of granite; buff to pink.
- **Obsidian**: glassy; silica-rich; usually black; conchoidal fracture; chemically equivalent to granite.
- **Pumice**: glassy + vesicular; floats on water.

**Intermediate:**
- **Andesite**: aphanitic; medium gray; often porphyritic.
- **Diorite**: phaneritic equivalent of andesite; salt-and-pepper; no visible quartz.

**Mafic:**
- **Basalt**: aphanitic; very dark; most common extrusive rock; oceanic crust, Hawaiian Islands.
- **Gabbro**: phaneritic equivalent of basalt; major part of oceanic crust.

**Ultramafic:**
- **Peridotite**: olivine dominant; upper mantle composition.

**Pyroclastic:**
- **Tuff**: consolidated volcanic ash.
- **Welded tuff**: fused ash; hot enough to stick together.
- **Volcanic breccia**: particles >ash-sized.

## Intrusive Structures (Plutons)
- **Dike**: tabular, discordant (cuts across bedding); transports magma upward.
- **Sill**: tabular, concordant (parallel to bedding); may show **columnar jointing** (6-sided shrinkage columns).
- **Batholith**: largest body (>100 km²); typically felsic to intermediate.
- **Stock**: smaller batholith (<100 km²).
- **Laccolith**: concordant mass that arches overlying strata upward.
- **Xenolith**: block of country rock suspended in pluton (assimilation product).
