Strong AI and weak AI are the two basic *stances* on what AI research is for. The distinction is not about how capable a system is — it is about what claim you are making when you build one. Strong AI claims the machine actually thinks; weak AI treats the machine as a model or a tool and makes no such claim. Course reference: B&S (2003), pp. 6–7.

## The Two Stances

**Strong AI**
1. Build a machine capable of thought, consciousness, and emotions equalling that of humans.
2. Humans are no more than elaborate computers.
3. The model *is* the mind.

**Weak AI**
1. Develop theories of human and animal intelligence, then test those theories by building working models — usually computer programs or robots.
2. The working models serve as a tool to aid understanding.
3. Does **not** claim the machines themselves are capable of thought, consciousness, and emotions.

## Problems With Each

| Stance | Problem |
|--------|---------|
| Strong AI | Hard to determine whether something is *really* consciously intelligent — this is the **other minds problem** in philosophy. |
| Weak AI | Utility of the result is limited by the stated goal. Hard to achieve general usefulness as in true intelligence. |

The other-minds problem is the sharp edge: you cannot verify consciousness from the outside, in a machine or in another person. This is why the [[Turing Test]] substitutes indistinguishable *behavior* for the unobservable inner state, and why Searle's [[Chinese Room]] attacks exactly that substitution.

## Exam Framing

Midterm Practice Problem 1 gives the strong-AI definition verbatim — "build machines capable of thought, consciousness, and emotions equalling that of humans" — and asks which stance it is (**strong AI**), then asks whether ChatGPT has reached that level. The second half is explicitly open-ended; either answer earns credit if the explanation holds up. The useful move is to argue from the other-minds problem: fluent behavior is compatible with both having and lacking inner states, so behavioral evidence alone cannot settle it.
