## Content Overview

Covers propositional logic, predicate logic, and proof techniques:

- **Propositional logic:** connectives (¬, ∧, ∨, →, ↔), truth tables, logical equivalences
- **Predicate logic:** quantifiers (∀, ∃), scope, negation of quantified statements
- **Proof techniques:**
  - Direct proof
  - Proof by contrapositive
  - Proof by contradiction
  - Mathematical induction (weak and strong)

## Key Result (from Exam 1 T/F)

> "For predicates A and B, if A ⇒ B then ¬B ⇒ ¬A" — **TRUE** (this is the contrapositive).

The contrapositive (¬B ⇒ ¬A) is logically equivalent to the original implication (A ⇒ B). This is distinct from the converse (B ⇒ A) and the inverse (¬A ⇒ ¬B), which are not generally equivalent.
