This transcript applies the [[Effective Annual Rate]] formula to a typical payday loan: borrow $100, repay $115 in two weeks ($15 fee). The result — an EAR over 3,600% — illustrates why the EAR is the correct measure of true borrowing cost.

## Calculation

**Biweekly periodic rate:**
```
r_biweekly = 115/100 − 1 = 0.15 = 15%
```

**Periods per year (biweekly):**
```
M = 52 weeks / 2 = 26 periods
```

**Effective Annual Rate:**
```
EAR = (1 + 0.15)^26 − 1 ≈ 36.86 = 3,686%
```

## Comparison with Mafia Loan

The Mafia loan (2% weekly, see [[M3: Mafia Loan]]) has an EAR of ~180%, far lower than the payday lender's 3,686%. The instructor poses this as a discussion question — why would a legal lender charge more than a criminal one?

## Key Takeaway

APR figures quoted by payday lenders (e.g., "15% fee") dramatically understate the true annual cost of borrowing. The EAR is the only apples-to-apples comparison metric for loans with different compounding frequencies.
