## Content Overview

Covers the foundational mathematical notation used throughout CSCE 411:

- **Sets:** notation, membership, subset relations, set operations (union, intersection, complement, power set)
- **Functions:** domain, codomain, range; injective, surjective, bijective functions
- **Sequences and tuples:** ordered structures, Cartesian products
- **Relations:** binary relations, equivalence relations, partial orders

These definitions are assumed knowledge in all subsequent chapters and are referenced directly in proofs, algorithm descriptions, and complexity arguments.
