This short transcript introduces Module 11's accounting review, assigned alongside Chapter 2 of the textbook. The instructor stresses that while this is a finance course, accounting literacy is essential for the course's most important skill: generating free cash flow projections for investment decisions.

## Key Message

Cash flow projections (used in [[Net Present Value]] analysis) are generated from accounting projections. To do DCF analysis — the most important function of a financial manager — you must be able to read and interpret financial statements. Students who skip this module will struggle with Module 12 ([[Free Cash Flow]]) and beyond.

## Coverage

Module 11 is a review of basic accounting concepts from the textbook, including:
- Reading income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements
- Accrual accounting principles
- How accounting measures differ from cash flows
