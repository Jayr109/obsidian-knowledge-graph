Calvin Beideman is the instructor for [[CSCE 411]] (Design and Analysis of Algorithms). His research lineage traces back to Robert Floyd, co-inventor of the [[Floyd-Warshall]] algorithm.

## Academic Lineage

As shared in Lecture 22 (a "fun fact"):

```
Robert Floyd
  └── Ronald Rivest
        └── Avrim Blum
              └── Santosh Vempala
                    └── Karthekeyan Chandrasekaran
                          └── Calvin Beideman
```

Robert Floyd is the same Floyd of [[Floyd-Warshall]], making Beideman an intellectual descendant of the very algorithm covered in his course.
