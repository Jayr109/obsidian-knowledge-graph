Sandeep Kumar is the primary instructor for [[CSCE 313]] (Introduction to Computer Systems) at Texas A&M University, and delivered most of the CSCE 313 lectures in this knowledge base.

## Courses Taught

- [[CSCE 313]] — Introduction to Computer Systems

Lecture 22, on network security and the data link layer, was delivered by [[Houngninou]] rather than Kumar.

## Course Materials

The CSCE 313 lectures introduce the operating system as the layer between hardware and applications — its three roles as illusionist, referee, and glue — and build toward processes, virtual memory, and the POSIX API.

- **Textbooks**: OSSTEP (*Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces*) and APUE (*Advanced Programming in the UNIX Environment*)
- Some slides are adapted from CS162 at UC Berkeley (Crooks & Zaharia)
