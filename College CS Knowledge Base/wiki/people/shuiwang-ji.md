Shuiwang Ji is a faculty member in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Texas A&M University and the instructor for [[CSCE 421]] (Machine Learning).

## Courses Taught

- [[CSCE 421]] — Machine Learning

## Course Materials

The CSCE 421 lecture decks are based on *Learning From Data* (Abu-Mostafa, Magdon-Ismail & Lin). Ji also authored the supplementary typed notes *Logistic Regression: From Binary to Multi-Class*, compiled by Yaochen Xie, which carry the full derivations for the softmax and cross-entropy gradients that the slides deliberately leave out.

The notes' acknowledgements credit support from NSF, NIH, and DARPA grants — the research funding behind the group that produced them.
