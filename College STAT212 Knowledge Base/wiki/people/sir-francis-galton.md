Sir Francis Galton (1822–1911) was an English scientist, cousin of Charles Darwin, and an early pioneer of statistics credited with some of the earliest work on **regression** and **correlation**. He coined the phrase "nature vs. nurture."

## Relevance to the course

- Collected the famous data set on the heights of **1,078 father–son pairs**, the running example in [[Simple Linear Regression Model|Notes 1]].
- His observation that sons' heights **regress toward the overall mean** (the line-of-averages slope is < 1) is the origin of the term *regression*.
- Galton fit: $\hat y = 33.887 + 0.514x$; $R^2 = 0.251$; slope 95% CI $(0.461, 0.567)$.

## Other people referenced in Notes 1
- **Carl Friedrich Gauss** (1777–1855) — credited with least squares ([[Least Squares Estimation]]).
- **Isaac Newton** / **Galileo** — the deterministic gravity example ($y = \sqrt{2x/g}$).
