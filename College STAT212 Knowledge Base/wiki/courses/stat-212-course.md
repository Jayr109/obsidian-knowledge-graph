STAT 212 is a college mathematical-statistics course focused on **regression, ANOVA, categorical data, and nonparametric methods**, combining rigorous derivations with applied analysis in R.

## Topics (from the 7 note sets)

1. Simple Linear Regression and Correlation
2. More General Regression (transformations)
3. Multiple Linear Regression
4. One Way ANOVA
5. Two Way ANOVA
6. Categorical Data
7. Nonparametric Methods

## Tools & conventions

- **Software:** R (`lm`, `summary`, `confint`, `predict`, `cor.test`, `qqnorm`, `density`).
- **Textbook:** referenced for correlation (§4.5.2) and prediction (pp. 441–442).
- **Homework:** derivations must be **handwritten** with a side-justification tag on every step (see the knowledge base `CLAUDE.md` Derivation Format and the [[Rules of Expectation and Variance]]).
