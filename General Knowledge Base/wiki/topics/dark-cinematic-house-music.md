A loosely defined aesthetic cluster in electronic music combining dark, cinematic tension with melodic house and ambient elements. The sound is characterized by minor keys, sweeping synth textures, dramatic builds, and emotional weight — simultaneously club-ready and introspective.

## Sub-Vibes

### Dark Cinematic / Epic
Heavy atmosphere, orchestral or cinematic production, often pairing hip-hop or R&B vocals with electronic production. Emotional scale and gravitas.

Representative tracks:
- *Pray For Me* — The Weeknd & Kendrick Lamar
- *Lost in the Fire* — Gesaffelstein & The Weeknd
- *Starboy* — The Weeknd & Daft Punk
- *The Hills* — The Weeknd
- *Colossal* — Tale Of Us *(melodic techno; minimal but spine-chilling)*

### Melodic House / Tech House
Driving rhythmic energy with emotional melodic layers. Prominent in the underground club scene; blends warmth with tension. John Summit is the clearest entry point for this sound.

Representative tracks:
- *Shadows* — John Summit
- *Lights Go Out* — John Summit
- *Sweet Disposition (Remix)* — The Temper Trap, John Summit & Silver Panda
- *Shiver (Cassian Remix)* — HAYLA, John Summit & Cassian
- *Turn On The Lights again..* — Swedish House Mafia, Future & Fred again.. *(feat. Anyma)*
- *Last Night* — Anyma & Layton Giordani
- *You & Me* — Max Styler
- *Flood* — Sebastian Ingrosso

### Ethereal / Ambient / Dreamy
Slower, more textural. Balearic influences. Prioritizes immersion and atmosphere over dancefloor energy.

Representative tracks:
- *I Could Be The One* (Ambient) — Avicii & Nicky Romero
- *Pandora* — Inan
- *i feel it coming* — The Weeknd & Daft Punk
- *Key103* — Floating Points
- *Nightfire* — Drua
- *Menace* — Hozho *(minimal techno with dark atmosphere)*
