This is the General Knowledge Base — a personal wiki for capturing and connecting knowledge across any domain: history, science, philosophy, technology, culture, literature, and more.

## Purpose

This wiki is built to accumulate understanding over time. Each ingest adds pages; each page links to others; over time a web of connected ideas emerges. The goal is not comprehensive coverage but deep, personally curated synthesis.

## How It Works

- **Ingest** a source (book, article, lecture, note) and the LLM extracts concepts, people, events, and works into individual wiki pages.
- **Query** the wiki to retrieve synthesized answers with citations.
- **Lint** periodically to find gaps, orphans, and contradictions.
