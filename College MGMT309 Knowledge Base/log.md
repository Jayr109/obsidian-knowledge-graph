**The synthesis worth recording.** The chapter is not a list of five independent perspectives. The Classical Management Perspective is given three specific limitations, and **each one generates a successor school**:

| Classical limitation | Successor |
|---|---|
| Slighted the role of the individual | Behavioral Management Perspective |
| Proposed universal guidelines that do not fit every organization | Contingency Perspective |
| Focused on stable, simple organizations | Systems Perspective (organizations are *open*) |

The Quantitative Management Perspective is the fourth branch — same classical ambition, better tooling. This chain-of-corrections reading is the organizing idea of the overview page and makes the "...Today" critique sections load-bearing rather than filler.

The **Hawthorne Studies** are the empirical hinge: a work group under piecework capped its own output and punished *rate busters* as well as *chiselers*. Sanctioning overproduction is what falsifies the economic-incentive model behind Taylor's system — that detail is the one to remember.
