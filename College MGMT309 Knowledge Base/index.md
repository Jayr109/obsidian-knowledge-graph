## Concepts
- [[Management]] — activities directed at resources to achieve goals efficiently and effectively; efficient vs. effective
- [[Organization]] — group working in a structured, coordinated fashion toward shared goals
- [[Kinds of Managers]] — three levels (top/middle/first-line) × six functional areas; the dimensions cross-cut
- [[Basic Management Functions]] — planning & decision making, organizing, leading, controlling; a cycle, not a line
- [[Fundamental Management Skills]] — technical, interpersonal, conceptual, diagnostic, communication, decision-making, time management
- [[The Science and Art of Management]] — rational/systematic vs. intuitive/experiential; managers need both
- [[Contemporary Management Challenges]] — globalization, ethics, quality, service economy, diversity, change, social media

## Frameworks
- [[The Evolution of Management Thought]] — the map: three historical + two contemporary perspectives as a chain of corrections
- [[Classical Management Perspective]] — scientific (individual worker) + administrative (total organization); three limitations
- [[Behavioral Management Perspective]] — attitudes, behaviors, group processes; human relations; organizational behavior
- [[Hawthorne Studies]] — the empirical hinge; group norms beat piecework; rate busters and chiselers
- [[Theory X and Theory Y]] — McGregor's contrasting assumptions about workers; Theory X aligns with scientific management
- [[Quantitative Management Perspective]] — management science + operations management; models, cost-effectiveness, computers
- [[Systems Perspective]] — system, open/closed, subsystem, synergy, entropy; organizations are open
- [[Contingency Perspective]] — appropriate behavior depends on the situation; rejects the universal perspective

## People
- [[Frederick W. Taylor]] — scientific management; soldiering; the piecework pay system
- [[Henri Fayol]] — administrative management; systematized management practice to make it teachable
- [[Max Weber]] — bureaucracy; foundation of contemporary organization theory
- [[Hugo Münsterberg]] — father of industrial psychology; selection, training, motivation
- [[Mary Parker Follett]] — anticipated the behavioral management perspective
- [[Douglas McGregor]] — Theory X and Theory Y; *The Human Side of Enterprise* (1960)
- [[Ricky W. Griffin]] — author of *Fundamentals of Management*, the course textbook
