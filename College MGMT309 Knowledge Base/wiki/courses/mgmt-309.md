MGMT 309 is a survey of management, built on Ricky W. Griffin's *Fundamentals of Management*, 11th edition (Cengage Learning, 2026). The course introduces what managers do, the skills the job requires, the historical schools of management thought, and the contemporary conditions managers operate under.

## The Shape of the Material

The course is **definition-heavy**. Chapter 1 alone introduces several taxonomies that must be reproduced complete and in order:

- Three levels of management, and six functional areas
- Four management functions
- Seven fundamental management skills
- Three historical perspectives and two contemporary ones
- Six systems terms

A recurring structural device is worth knowing in advance: each historical perspective is presented **with its own critique** — a "...Today" assessment of what survives and what does not. Those critiques are not filler. They explain why each subsequent school exists, and they are exam-relevant in their own right.
