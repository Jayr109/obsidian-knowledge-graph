Ricky W. Griffin is the author of *Fundamentals of Management*, the textbook used in [[MGMT 309]]. The material in this knowledge base derives from the **11th edition** (Cengage Learning, 2026).

## The Textbook's Structure

Chapter 1, *Understanding the Manager's Job*, sets a pattern the rest of the book follows:

- Each chapter opens with numbered **learning objectives** that map one-to-one onto its major sections.
- Historical perspectives are each paired with a **"...Today"** assessment of what survives and what does not.
- Key terms are defined explicitly and set off from the surrounding discussion.

The objectives are worth using directly as a study checklist — they state exactly what the chapter expects the reader to be able to do.
