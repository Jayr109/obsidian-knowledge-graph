Mary Parker Follett (1868–1933) **anticipated the behavioral management perspective**, working on the human and social dimensions of organizations before the school that would formalize those concerns existed.

## Contribution

The chapter's characterization is that Follett came *ahead of* the [[Behavioral Management Perspective]] rather than within it. Her concerns — the individual and the group inside the organization — were exactly those the [[Classical Management Perspective]] was neglecting during the same period.

She is grouped with [[Hugo Münsterberg]] as an early contributor whose work preceded the [[Hawthorne Studies]], the empirical event that made the behavioral perspective mainstream.
